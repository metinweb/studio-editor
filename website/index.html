FROM THOUGHT TO DOCUMENT

The tools you need,
right where you write.

From everyday notes to detailed content, all in one workspace.

01

Find your writing flow

Headings, task lists, slash commands and Markdown shortcuts. Find your way through long documents with a live outline.

/ commands · @ mentions
02

Take control of tables

Select multiple cells, merge, resize columns and format in bulk. Paste tables from Excel and Google Sheets.

Cell formatting · TSV paste
03

Tell your story visually

Add images from your media library, then crop, rotate and adjust their colors. Work with video, audio and PDF files too.

Built-in image editing
04

A place for every draft

Favorites, tags and full-text search. Keep going with autosave, persistent versions and full workspace backups.

Local storage · Version history
05

Make room for feedback

Add comments to text, reply and resolve them. Suggest edits to a selection, then accept or reject each change.

In-document comments and suggestions
06

Ready to share your work

Open the HTML source, preview and export your work. Download a DOCX file or print to PDF from your browser.

HTML · DOCX · Print to PDF

editor together.

Studio Editor is an evolving beta. Contribute a bug report, an improvement or an idea worth exploring.

Real-time collaboration is experimental. Automatic track changes and DOCX import are not yet supported. The demo stores documents locally; back up your work before clearing browser data.
